Klotski is an MoE inference engine that reduces pipeline bubbles with an expert-aware multi-batch pipeline, a constraint-sensitive I/O–compute planner, and a correlation-aware expert prefetcher. Experiments report up to **85.12×** throughput improvement over prior offloading baselines.

I co-designed the expert-aware prefetch method and the expert offload mechanism as part of the CAAI–MindSpore academic research program.
