Fate speeds up Mixture-of-Experts inference on edge and resource-constrained devices with a cross-layer gate, reducing expert-loading overhead.

I contributed as an industrial mentor through the CAAI–MindSpore academic research program, including guidance on unified-memory deployment and expert-prefetcher launch cost on constrained CPUs.
